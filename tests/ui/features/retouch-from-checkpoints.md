# Retouch from checkpoints
Reach: open a folder and fuse it (`--open`, `--fuse-tabs 0`, `--enter-editor`); each further result is another **Fuse stack** in the same tab after **STACKING** (`FFModeSwitch::stacking-half`), with the FUSE algorithm and the ALIGN **Enabled** switch changed on the dock's **Settings** tab. In the editor the Images dock lists the results above the frames; a click on a result row picks it as the brush source.
Sub-features: the left pane label "Checkpoint · <algorithm> <time>" in place of the frame name, a stroke painted from a result and saved as a checkpoint (row text "· 1 stroke"), Alt+click on that stroke picking its source result back, the "N hidden: reason" line refusing a click, the delete question counting the strokes that use a result, a deleted result gone from both lists while its stroke stays, **Save project** clearing every UNSAVED mark. The results group, its pills, collapse and the keyboard stepping are in `images-panel.md`.
Ids: `FFImageRow::area` N (rows on screen in visible order, starting under the heading "Results"; the hidden line counts, headings and rows scrolled out of the list do not), `FFSectionHeading::root` N (headings in view), `FFCanvas::pane-left` (the label), `FFCanvas::pane-right` (the Composite), `FFCheckpointRow::area` N (newest first), `FFCheckpointRow::trash`, `FFDock::checkpoint-panel`, `AppWindow::checkpoint-delete`, `AppWindow::divergence-warning`, `FFToggle::area` 0 (ALIGN **Enabled**), `FFMenu::root` 0 (FUSE **Algorithm**), `FFMenuList::option-area` 2 (**Focus map**), `FFTabStrip::projects-button`, `FFProjectsRail::save-project`, `AppWindow::projects-overlay`.
Gotchas:
- Build order matters: Details (**Keep**), then a fuse with ALIGN off (no keep question; it becomes the edited state), then ALIGN on with "Focus map" (**Keep**), then the stroke and **Save checkpoint**. The Images dock lists only results that share the edited state's pixel grid, so the ALIGN-off result is the hidden one ("1 hidden: different alignment"); built in another order, the other results read as hidden. A fuse over unsaved strokes asks "Save your edits first?", so the stroke comes last.
- Every fuse of `fixtures/mantis-512` takes up to two minutes on a debug build; a stroke drag takes about 9 s.
- The left pane label is cut at 300 px: quote only "Checkpoint · Details" or "Checkpoint · Focus map". It reads "· 1 stroke" at its end for the retouched result.
- Selecting a frame (Press Down, the delete falling back to a frame) scrolls the Images dock, so the first result row and the heading "Results" can leave the tree; `FFImageRow::area` and `FFSectionHeading::root` then count from the next row or heading. Clicking the heading "8 frames" returns the list to its top.
- The CHECKPOINTS list names a retouched row "Retouch on <algorithm>". Its trash icon exists only while the pointer rests on a row and the list holds 2 or more rows (hover again after any other click).
- A deleted result that a stroke uses is hidden and kept: it leaves the CHECKPOINTS list and the results group, the stroke keeps its pixels, and the brush source falls back to the selected frame. Alt+click on that stroke then changes neither the selection nor the label.
- The editor toolbar's **Save checkpoint** is a plain button with no id of its own: the journey clicks it with `click "Save checkpoint" AppWindow::editor-toolbar` and reads its enabled state, and the undo arrow's, with `buttons AppWindow::editor-toolbar`. A **Save project** also saves a pending stroke as a checkpoint.
- **Save project** writes `frames.ffp` into the launch's `--save-folder` with no chooser. It clears the "UNSAVED" pills and the tab marker; the project card then reads one checkpoint fewer than were made (the hidden one is not counted).
- Human only, no driver can read them: the brush ring's colour and its small layers glyph over a chosen result (journey 24, step 14), Ctrl+wheel stepping through the result rows first and then the frames with the label following in step 28, painting from a different result covering a stroke and painting from the base restoring it in steps 29 and 31 (pixel judgements, no pixel compare), the overlay's colours over a stroke painted from a result, and the preview pictures in Image list and Large previews being drawn from the results and not the frames.
