# Analytics
Reach: press the Analytics button in the icon bar on the left edge, directly under the step calculator button (label **Analytics**, inside `AppWindow::icon-bar`). It is never greyed out: with no project open it shows the waiting panel. The same button, **Close** (top right of the overlay) and Escape close it. `--open <folder> --analytics` opens it once the folder has loaded; `--analytics-hold-ms MS` holds each count back so the pending rows can be looked at.
Sub-features: the overlap bar block above the list ("SHARPNESS OVERLAP", its caption, the legend "0 %" / "5 %" / "20 %+", one segment per neighbour pair with the image numbers 1 to N on the boundaries); a finished segment shows its whole-percent share, a pair without any sharp area a hollow segment with a dash "–"; a hover tooltip of two lines (the two image numbers and file names over a verdict); while the count runs the bar is a full row of neutral segments with a soft band, no figure and no tooltip; a bar of only no-data segments is still drawn; one row per frame of the selected tab in the dock's order (chip, file name, bar, count; excluded frames are listed too); bar length is the count divided by the largest count, empty for every row when all counts are 0; no millimetre column without rail positions; without a project only the waiting panel "No stack loaded" / "Load a stack to analyse it.", with no heading, caption or bar; a one-frame stack shows only the panel "Not enough images"; while the count runs every row is listed with a sweeping bar and a spinner in place of its count (`pending`), then a growing fill with the spinner kept, then the final count; the count restarts for the newly selected tab; closing the overlay stops the count; one full-canvas overlay at a time (the projects overview, the notification center and the step calculator close it, and it closes them).
Ids: `AppWindow::icon-bar` (the button is clicked by its label), `AppWindow::analytics-overlay` (**Close** is clicked by label inside it), `FFOverlapBar::area` (the bar's hover area; its centre is the middle pair, a window point picks another).
Gotchas:
- The overlay covers the canvas only: the toolbar, the dock and the bottom bar stay visible. The window's title-bar button is also labelled "Close", so a Close step names `AppWindow::analytics-overlay`.
- Counts of `fixtures/mantis-512/frames` (00.tif to 07.tif): 0, 0, 4, 11, 0, 0, 0, 1. `fixtures/mantis-64/frames` counts 0 for all three frames.
- Overlap bar of `fixtures/mantis-512/frames`, pairs 1-2 to 7-8: "0 %", "0 %", "0 %", "0 %", "–" (5-6, no sharp area in either image), "0 %", "100 %". The pairs 1-2, 2-3, 6-7 and 7-8 have fewer than 10 sharp areas between them and are drawn dimmed. `fixtures/mantis-64/frames` gives two "–" segments (numbers 1 to 3). Tooltip of pair k: "Images k and k+1 · 0(k-1).tif, 0k.tif" over "0 % – likely a gap in focus here" (3-4, 4-5), "0 % – too few sharp areas to be sure" (1-2, 2-3, 6-7), "100 % – too few sharp areas to be sure" (7-8) or "No sharp areas in either image" (5-6).
- The segments, their texts and the numbers are not buttons: `buttons` lists nothing of the bar, so the steps read a screenshot. The tooltip shows only once the count is final and the pointer has rested on a segment; hover `FFOverlapBar::area` at a window point (in the default window the bar spans x 64 to 924 at y 185, so on 8 images pair k has its centre at x 64 + 122.9 (k - 0.5): pair 5-6 at 617,185); the tooltip's first line names the pair that was hit. After a tab switch from the waiting panel the segments are drawn about a second late.
- Segment colours (red at 0 %, yellow at 5 %, green from 20 %) are judged by eye: only `(human only)` steps name them.
- With the hold flag every opening shows the pending rows (sweep and spinner, no number) for the hold time; a step that reads rows says `(within 60 s)`.
- Not covered by the journey: the failed state (red panel "Analysis failed" over the core message, no bar and no rows; a frame deleted after the folder was opened, reachable only by deleting a file during the hold), the one-frame panel "Not enough images" (no checked-in frames folder holds one image), the thinning of the bar's numbers on long stacks, the bar's entry and sweep motion, the partial fills (a single-tile fixture goes from pending straight to final; `--analytics-tile-size 96` with `--analytics-tile-pause-ms` makes them watchable), a list taller than the body with its scrollbar (needs a stack of more frames than fits, none is checked in, and the wheel is a human-only input), and the editor mode. Check them by hand with a copy of a fixture under `.scratch/`.
- Opening the overlay with a driver needs a build with the `mcp` feature (`tools/gui/run-gui.sh` builds it); a binary built without it answers no request.
