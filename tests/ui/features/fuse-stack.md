# Fuse a stack
Reach: open a folder with `Start: --open fixtures/mantis-64/frames` (3 frames of 64x64, finishes in seconds) or `fixtures/mantis-512/frames` (8 frames of 512x512, about 20 s), then press **Fuse stack** (`FFToolbar::fuse-button`). `--fuse-tabs 0` presses it on tab 0 at launch; the run is usually over (the Keep question waiting) before a step can look at it, unless `--slow-run N` holds it.
Sub-features: the not-fused state and what stays unavailable before a fuse (export, overlay), keep or discard the result (`AppWindow::divergence-warning`), a second kept result, fuse at launch, pause and resume, cancel, queued tab (it runs while another tab waits at its Keep question: journey 17), the record of a cancelled run in the projects overview and **Run again**, retry after a failure (`FFToolbar::retry-button`, journey 16 steps 18-19, listed under open-and-refusals).
Ids: launch options `--slow-run N` (hold the Nth run started in the launch, counted from 1, in its fuse stage) and `--slow-run-seconds S` (how long, default 150); toolbar `FFToolbar::fuse-button`, `pause-button`, `resume-button`, `cancel-button`, `cancelling-button`, `retry-button`; bottom bar `FFBottomBar::status-text` (the stage word, "Paused", "No job running", "fused HH:MM"), `step-text`, `eta-text`, `progress-bar`, `overlay-toggle`; `FFModeSwitch::stacking-half`; the right dock's CHECKPOINTS list with `FFCheckpointPanel::empty-note` and `checkpoint-export`; tabs `FFTab::area` N (N counts from 0 in opening order); the projects overview `FFTabStrip::projects-button`, cards `FFProjectCard::card-area`.
Gotchas:
- `buttons AppWindow::toolbar` lists the toolbar's buttons (it exists in stacking mode, not in the editor); a greyed button's line starts with `x`. `buttons FFDock::checkpoint-panel` does the same for **Export image**.
- A slowed run (`--slow-run N`) holds in its fuse stage for at least 150 s of running time, pauses excluded; the other runs of the launch keep their speed. Brightness matching and alignment come first and are not held: Pause is only guaranteed once the bottom bar reads "Fusing", so a step that pauses waits for that word. Pause, Resume and Cancel act within seconds from there. Journey 02 slows run 4 (launch run 1, step 8, step 12, then step 17); journey 09 slows run 1.
- Both fixtures end their fuse in the Keep/Discard dialog: with the default alignment the run leaves frames unregistered (for `mantis-512` the frames named `00.tif`, `01.tif`, `06.tif` and `07.tif`), and only then does the dialog appear. Journey 01 steps 5, 11, 15, 18 and journey 02 steps 1, 8, 12 depend on it; if the dialog does not appear, they fail. After **Keep** the app switches to the editor, so a journey clicks **STACKING** to see the CHECKPOINTS list; **Discard** leaves the mode alone.
- A modal blocks everything below it; **Fuse stack** stays visible behind it and a click on it does nothing (01 step 6).
- Before the first fuse, and after a discard, **Export image** is greyed and the **Overlay** toggle stays off. Both are read on the same screen as the "Not fused yet" card and the "None yet — fuse a stack" line.
- A second fuse after a kept result adds a second CHECKPOINTS row (01 step 17); a discarded fuse adds none (01 step 20).
- With ALIGN **Enabled** off a fuse commits with no Keep question: the bottom bar reads "fused" and a time, the app switches to the editor, and CHECKPOINTS gains a row (01 steps 21-24).
- A real failed run: `fixtures/open-rejected` opens as 3 frames (the row of `02.tif` reads "Preview unavailable") and **Fuse stack** ends in the bottom bar status "Failed" with **Retry** (`FFToolbar::retry-button`) in place of **Fuse stack** (this id exists only in that state, so `click-id FFToolbar::fuse-button` finds nothing then), the bell gains one "Fusion failed" record and a toast shows only while the window has focus. The run fails in under 5 s and no `--slow-run` is needed. **Retry** runs it again and ends "Failed" once more (second record; no Keep question, no composite): 16 steps 18-19. `--toolbar-state failed` shows only a preview with no job behind it (**Retry**; status "Failed", "ETA —"); `--toolbar-state running` previews "Aligning", "42 of 61", "ETA 1:12". The previews `paused` and `cancelling` ("Cancelling…" greyed) exist too.
- Pause, resume, queued and cancel are driven steps (02 steps 17-25) done within the one slowed run started at step 17. A run that is not slowed ends in about 20 s on `mantis-512` and a read-and-click step takes about 10 s, so it cannot be watched step by step.
- A fuse that ends in the Keep question belongs to its tab: with `--fuse-tabs 0` the question is raised for tab 0 and shows only while tab 0 is selected, and clicking a tab or **Projects** while it is open does nothing (02 steps 2, 3). The second tab stays unfused, with its own question later (02 step 12: "00.tif and 02.tif could not be aligned").
- Tab order is `--open` order: `FFTab::area` 0 is `mantis-512` (8 frames), 1 is `mantis-64` (3 frames).
- **Fuse stack** reads "Queued" on an idle tab while another tab runs (02 step 19) and "Fuse stack" again once nothing runs. Cancel the queued tab before the running one: cancelling the running one lets the queued tab start. With `--fuse-tabs 0,1` tab 1 is queued, starts as soon as tab 0 reaches its Keep question and runs to an outcome of its own (on `mantis-64` its own question naming `00.tif` and `02.tif`); each question shows only while its tab is selected, and the launch leaves tab 1 selected, so the question seen first after a settled launch is tab 1's. Journey 17 follows this with `--slow-run 2`; journey 02 starts only tab 0 so its later steps have one run to pause and queue.
- `--fuse-tabs 0,0` presses **Fuse stack** twice on tab 0: the second press waits until the first run's Keep question is answered, so the bottom bar reads "No job running" while that question is up; after **Keep** the second run starts on its own and asks its own question (04 steps 1-3).
- Cancelling shows a disabled "Cancelling…" button for a moment before **Fuse stack** returns. The cancelled card's stage line reads "STOPPED WHILE FUSING" (or the stage the run was in), so step 22 checks only its start; a cancelled tab with an earlier kept result still reads "8 frames · 1 checkpoint". **Run again** keeps the card "Cancelled" until its run's result is kept: the re-run ends in the Keep question (02 step 23) and the card changes only after **Keep** (02 step 24).
- **Run again** has no id of its own; it sits in the card inside `AppWindow::projects-overlay` and is found by its label (`click "Run again" AppWindow::projects-overlay`).
- Opening a folder raises the stack-grouping question only when its frames fall into several stacks (capture times more than 60 s apart). The mantis fixtures are one stack, so no such question is expected.
