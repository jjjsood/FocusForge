# Open and refusals
Reach: open inputs with `Start: --open <folder or .ffp>`. The flag is repeatable and each path is opened in order, so one launch can mix successful opens with a refusal; the native **Open folder…** chooser and dropping a folder are human only. A load runs on a worker; every question, refusal and notice below appears within a few seconds of launch over the opened tabs or the empty window.
Sub-features: refusal "No frames here" (`AppWindow::refusal-dialog`, button "Pick another folder…"); refusal "This project cannot be opened" (same dialog, button "Close"); refusal "These are not the project's frames" (same dialog, after **Locate frames…** on the wrong folder); grouping question (`AppWindow::grouping-question`: "This folder reads as N stacks of M frames.", "Open as one project", "Open as N projects", "Cancel"); skipped-subfolder record ("Some images were skipped", message "N subfolder(s) skipped" with the paths, shown as a toast and in the notification center); fingerprint mismatch (`AppWindow::mismatch-dialog`: "The source frames are not the same", Cancel, "Locate frames…", "Open anyway"); missing sources (`AppWindow::sources-missing-dialog`: "The frames are no longer there", "Close", "Locate frames…"); crash record ("Previous session stopped", a toast and a notification-center row with **Acknowledge** and **Diagnostics**, covered by 15-notification-center.md steps 29-35); loading indicator ("Loading…"); RAW folder as a one-frame project; compressed TIFF variants; the empty start after the last tab closes.
Ids: the mismatch and sources-missing dialogs are click targets by label within their `AppWindow::` id (`click "Open anyway" AppWindow::mismatch-dialog`); the refusal's **Close** and the grouping question's buttons are plain rectangles that `buttons` does not list and `click` by label does not find, so they are clicked by id: `FFRefusalDialog::pick-area` (Escape closes any refusal as well, with no click), `FFGroupingQuestion::one-button`, `many-button` and `cancel-area`; `FFTabStrip::projects-button`, `FFTabStrip::left-arrow`; `FFTab::area` N and `FFTab::close-area` N (N counts from 0 in opening order, also for tabs scrolled out of the strip); the Projects overview's scrollbar is `FFScrollbar::track-area` instance 1 (instance 0 belongs to the Images list); a toast is `AppWindow::toast`, its close glyph `FFToast::close` (**Dismiss notification**) and its body `FFToast::card`; one toast shows at a time, so each has one match, number 0; the bell is `FFIconBar::bell`.
Gotchas:
- Every dialog here is modal and has exactly the buttons listed: the dimmed area and **Projects** do nothing while it is up .
- A folder with no frames (`fixtures/retouch`) gives the dialog "No frames here" and also a "Not a project" card (titled with the folder name, "LEFT ALONE", "not read · not changed") in the Projects overlay, whose count then reads "N · 1 did not open" with N counting the open projects too. It never gives a card alone. Its button **Pick another folder…** opens the system chooser on the window's own thread (human only, and nothing after it can be driven until the chooser is cancelled); the other refusals' button is "Close" and just dismisses. Pressing Escape closes any refusal and opens no chooser, so journey 12 launches with `fixtures/retouch` last among its `--open` paths and dismisses the dialog with `Press Escape` (12 step 3); the card stays after the dialog, also after every tab is closed (journey 12 steps 4 and 18). The refusal stands over the Projects overview too: Escape closes the refusal first and the overview stays open.
- The failure card stays after every tab is closed.
- A folder holding `frames/` is read through it, and its other subfolders are never walked (so `fixtures/mantis-64`, `mantis-512` with `golden/`, and every `mantis-64-<compression>` variant open with no notice and no card). Reference TIFFs beside `frames/` are ignored.
- `fixtures/blackmagic-micro-dng` opens as a one-frame project named "frame" (card second line "1 frame · no checkpoint yet"), not as a refusal: RAW extensions count as frames; the refusal text names "16-bit TIFF" and "camera RAW" as the readable formats. Journey 12 steps 5-7 assert the pane label "frame.dng" and the counts, not the picture (the pane stays black). **Fuse stack** is greyed with this one frame (step 8, read with `buttons AppWindow::toolbar`).
- The 24 `mantis-64-<codec>-<p1|p2>-<strip|tile>-<ii|mm>` variants all hold `frames/00.tif`..`02.tif` plus `reference.tif`; one stands for all (journey 12 steps 9-14). The fuse ends in the Keep question like any fixture fuse.
- Opening several folders in one launch makes the last successful one the current tab; a refused one never changes the current tab. With `--open fixtures/blackmagic-micro-dng --open fixtures/mantis-64-lzw-p2-tile-mm` the cards read "frame" (1 frame) then "mantis-64-lzw-p2-tile-mm" (3 frames) and the "CURRENT" chip sits on the second; the overview stays open until a card or the **Projects** button closes it, so a step that clicks a card needs it open.
- A toast shows only while the window holds keyboard focus (otherwise the record becomes a desktop banner), so every toast step is human only; the same record is read through the bell and the notification center. An info toast stays 6 s, a crash toast 10 s, and pointer hover holds it.
- The loading indicator ("Loading…" with a spinner over the canvas) appears only for a load longer than 500 ms, and every fixture opens faster. `--slow-open S` holds each folder or project load for S seconds before it reads anything, so with `--open` three times the loads run one after the other and the indicator shows for 3 x S seconds in all. A click during a load does nothing new: Zoom in on the empty window leaves the readout at "—" (12 step 1). Every project that lands closes the Projects overlay, so 12 step 2 waits up to 90 s for the last load and the refusal, step 3 dismisses it and step 4 opens the overview.
- Not covered: grouping question **Cancel** and mismatch **Cancel**: each question is raised once per launch, and journey 16 needs the opening answer (**Open as 2 projects**, **Open anyway**) to get the tabs the later steps read, so a Cancel would drop that fixture and leave those steps without their tab; **Locate frames…** on the mismatch and sources-missing dialogs and the refusal it can raise ("These are not the project's frames") open the native folder chooser (human only); the crash toast's **Dismiss notification**.
- Journey 16 opens six fixtures in one launch, in this order: `fixtures/open-rejected`, `fixtures/open-root-and-subfolder`, `fixtures/projects/missing.ffp`, `fixtures/projects/mismatch.ffp`, `fixtures/open-two-stacks`, `fixtures/projects/corrupt.ffp`. The corrupt project goes last because every project that lands clears the refusal text, and the next `--open` starts without waiting for a dialog. The four dialogs then stand stacked, the refusal on top, then sources-missing, mismatch and the grouping question; the dimmed area and **Projects** do nothing while any is up, so each is dismissed from the top (steps 1-6). What each fixture gives: `open-rejected` is 3 frames whose `02.tif` is cut inside its strip data (the open succeeds, its row reads "Preview unavailable", **Fuse stack** ends "Failed" with **Retry** in under 5 s and **Retry** fails the same way, steps 17-19); `open-root-and-subfolder` is 3 frames in the root plus a `notes/` subfolder (one tab, the record "Some images were skipped", a "Not a project" card "notes"); `open-two-stacks` holds `a/` and `b/` of 3 frames each (the question "This folder reads as 2 stacks of 6 frames."); `projects/missing.ffp` and `mismatch.ffp` are valid projects over `projects/frames/` that resolve to nothing or to swapped frames, and each lands as a tab, a card "3 frames · 1 checkpoint"; `corrupt.ffp` gives "This file is not a FocusForge project." and no card. Nothing in the journey saves, so `fixtures/` stays unchanged.
- The Projects overview of that launch lists 7 cards (the count reads "7 · 1 did not open", the heading "Projects"): the last card, "Not a project" "notes", lies below the fold and is reached by dragging the scrollbar (16 step 8). The overview closes with every project that lands, so it is read only after the last load (16 step 7). The notification center is hidden while the overview is open (16 step 9 closes the overview before the bell in step 10).
Needs (not yet in `fixtures/`):
- Crash record: the launcher sets `FOCUSFORGE_DATA_DIR=<scratch>` (the app reads `<scratch>/focusforge/crashes/`) with one file `20260918T010203Z-focusforge-7.json` holding `{"format":1,"payload":"boom"}` and no `.reported` marker, as a copy per run: **Acknowledge** in the notification center writes `<stem>.reported` beside it, so the checked-in seed must not be the folder used. Every other run needs `FOCUSFORGE_DATA_DIR` pointing at an empty scratch folder, else a real crash left on the machine raises the crash record over it. Unlocks: **Diagnostics** on the record opens "Settings" on Diagnostics with the label "Save to".
